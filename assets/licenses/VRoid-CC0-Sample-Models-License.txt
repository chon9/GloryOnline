Embedded VRM meta of every shipped model (verified by assets/characters/anime/tools, see CREDITS.md):
  allowedUserName = Everyone, commercial use = Allow, and either licenseName = CC0
  or the VRoid Hub licence with redistribution = allow, modification = allow, credit = unnecessary
  (https://hub.vroid.com/license?allowed_to_use_user=everyone&characterization_allowed_user=everyone&corporate_commercial_use=allow&credit=unnecessary&modification=allow&personal_commercial_use=profit&redistribution=allow&sexual_expression=allow&version=1&violent_expression=allow)

Files (assets/characters/anime/*.glb + *.vrm.json) are MODIFIED versions: converted from VRM 0.x to glTF binary,
normal/matcap/outline/thumbnail textures dropped, textures downsized (512 px body, 1024 px face/hair), primitives
merged, a 180-degree facing fix added, humanoid + spring-bone data moved to the .vrm.json sidecar. At runtime the
materials are replaced by the project's toon shader and recoloured.
